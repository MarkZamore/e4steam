# Upstream attribution and notices

e4steam is created by Kamilchik, published from <https://github.com/Kamilhik/e4steam>, and declared Apache-2.0 by its author. This repository mirrors unmodified release binaries and records their original URLs; it does not create a forked Minecraft mod.

The exact upstream `LICENSE`, `NOTICE` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` files for `v0.3.4` and `v0.3.2` are preserved under `upstream-notices/<tag>/`. Their source URLs and SHA-256 are recorded in `upstream-notices/sources.json`. The repository-root LICENSE and NOTICE are copied verbatim from the current upstream release.

Each mirrored JAR is byte-for-byte identical to its upstream asset, retaining its embedded notices and licenses. This includes dependency notices and the SkinRestorer MIT notice bundled by the modern release. Those components retain their respective licenses; Apache-2.0 does not replace their notices. See the complete upstream notice documents rather than inferring all dependency licenses from the mod metadata.

No separate Public Servers addon, Minecraft client, Minecraft server, NeoForge/Forge loader binary or game asset is distributed here.
