Original author URLs, versions and hashes are retained independently of the downstream delivery coordinates.

The exact upstream `LICENSE`, `NOTICE` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` files for `v0.3.4` and `v0.3.2` are preserved under `upstream-notices/<tag>/`. The six modern profile derivatives change only the declared pause-menu class and mixin configuration and add a downstream modification notice. Those components retain their respective licenses; Apache-2.0 does not replace their notices. The pinned source, patch and exact byte boundary are documented in [NATIVE-UI.md](NATIVE-UI.md) and the frozen profile inventory.
