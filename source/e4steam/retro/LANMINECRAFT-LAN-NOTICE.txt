LANMinecraft downstream e4steam modifications

This artifact includes the e4steam project by Kamilchik and contributors,
https://github.com/Kamilhik/e4steam, licensed under the Apache License 2.0.
The upstream source revision is recorded in source/UPSTREAM.json in the
LANMinecraft e4steam source repository. The original license and third-party
notices remain bundled in META-INF.

LANMinecraft modifies the Forge 1.12.2 and Forge 1.16.5 integrations to
preserve saved integrated-world permissions on publication, show the native
one-click Steam sharing action and list the names of Steam-hosted worlds.
These changes are opt-in for launcher-managed worlds.

Modified source and build instructions: https://github.com/MarkZamore/e4steam
